## A-Priori AST Budget Justification
To strictly prevent p-hacking (where the AST budget is tuned until the desired equation is found), the ADCD grammar enumeration budget is fixed *a priori* using a structural, mathematical argument. 

The budget must be large enough to express a depth-3 composition of the most complex parameterized primitive in the dictionary, which is the Lorentz-like correction:
D_lor(x) = x / (sqrt(1 - x) * (1 + sqrt(1 - x)))

When SymPy expands a depth-2 composition D_lor(D_lor(x)) and assigns 	heta_i variables, the resulting AST contains exactly 36 tokens and reaches a depth of 10. To allow for a small safety margin for ratio construction and scaling coefficients, we set the absolute bounds to:
- **max_tokens = 50**
- **max_depth = 12**

This calculation is made *before* running any evaluation, completely divorcing the hyperparameter from empirical fitting results. The diagnostic script udget_sweep.py is for theoretical validation only and its output is not used to select these values.
