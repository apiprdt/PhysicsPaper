\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{ADCD} (Asymptotic Dictionary Correction Discovery), a
deterministic framework for recovering structured corrections to known physical laws
from noisy observational data.
ADCD operates in the correction-first paradigm: given a known classical baseline,
it searches exclusively for the anomalous residual that vanishes at the classical limit.

The framework rests on three interlocking innovations.
\emph{First}, algebraically regularized primitive functions enforce exact
asymptotic vanishing, eliminating catastrophic cancellation that corrupts
gradient-based optimization at classical limits.
\emph{Second}, a five-dimensional (MLT$\Theta$Q) Buckingham-$\pi$ engine
automatically derives physically valid dimensionless ratio arguments,
enabling the system to correctly handle electromagnetic and thermodynamic
variables without manual ratio specification.
\emph{Third}, a \emph{Phenomenon-Specific Taxonomy} formalizes the Bayesian prior
inherent in domain expertise: the primitive search space is restricted
to functional families whose derivation from first principles is documented in the
physics literature, preventing physically absurd candidates
(e.g., trigonometric oscillations of charge) without resorting to brute-force
enumeration.

Evaluated on three canonical physics scenarios under historical low-signal
observation windows, ADCD demonstrates distinct epistemic behaviors.
For Screened Coulomb ($\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = 30.65$) and Entropy Expansion
($\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = 14.58$), the system achieves full four-step validation
(IDENTIFIABLE), with the Screened Coulomb result constituting an \emph{exact}
symbolic match proved blind by virtue of an internal parameter index offset
($\theta_4$ rather than $\theta_0$).
For Time Dilation at $v \le 0.3c$, the Lorentz structure is recovered at Rank~1
but the system autonomously withholds the structural claim (WITHHELD) because
the historical data carry insufficient signal for confident identifiability.
This self-aware epistemic restraint is a core design property: ADCD is
engineered to report ``the data do not yet support a claim'' rather than
return a confident but unreliable answer.
All results are byte-exact deterministic across independent runs.

\vspace{0.3em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} symbolic regression; correction discovery;
dimensional analysis; Bayesian prior; Pareto front; model selection
\end{abstract}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Introduction}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

Physical laws often follow a correction-first structure: a known classical
baseline is extended by a term that vanishes at the classical
limit~\cite{einstein1905,debye1923,callen1985}.
Recovering that correction from observational data is a problem distinct from
full equation discovery: the baseline is fixed, only the residual must be found,
and the residual must vanish exactly at the boundary where classical physics holds.

General-purpose symbolic regression (SR) engines such as PySR~\cite{cranmer2023},
AI Feynman~\cite{udrescu2020}, and PhySO~\cite{tenachi2023} excel at discovering
equations from scratch, but face a cluster of difficulties in this correction-first
setting.
\emph{Catastrophic cancellation}: as the correction $\Delta \to 0$, floating-point
subtraction of large nearly-equal terms destroys the gradient
signal~\cite{ieee754}, causing the optimizer to receive misleading feedback
precisely where the physics is most constrained.
\emph{Stochastic irreproducibility}: evolutionary and Monte Carlo search
engines~\cite{bongard2007,schmidt2009} cannot guarantee byte-identical results
across runs, complicating audit and replication.
\emph{Silent overclaiming}: all major SR engines return a single best-fit equation
regardless of whether the data carry sufficient statistical power to distinguish
among competing structures~\cite{lacava2021}.

We present ADCD, a correction-first framework designed to address all three
failure modes simultaneously.
Its algebraically regularized primitives make exact asymptotic vanishing an
algebraic identity rather than a numerical approximation.
Its deterministic grammar enumeration guarantees reproducible, auditable search.
Its four-step validation protocol uses BIC-based Ablation Control to explicitly
distinguish identifiable from unidentifiable structural claims,
reporting WITHHELD when data are insufficient rather than overclaiming.

The central question motivating ADCD is not ``can a machine rediscover known
corrections?'' but rather: ``under what data conditions can a machine
\emph{reliably} distinguish the true correction from structurally similar
alternatives, and when must it honestly refuse?''

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Related Work}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

General SR engines (Eureqa~\cite{schmidt2009}, PySR~\cite{cranmer2023},
AI Feynman~\cite{udrescu2020,udrescu2020b}, PhySO~\cite{tenachi2023})
target unconstrained equation recovery and do not enforce classical-limit
constraints algebraically.
SINDy~\cite{brunton2016} shares our library-based approach but discovers
governing equations rather than corrections.
PINNs~\cite{raissi2019,karniadakis2021} embed physical laws as soft loss penalties;
ADCD imposes them as hard algebraic identities before any numerical computation.
AI-Descartes~\cite{cornelio2023} combines SR with axiomatic theory derivation,
but does not address catastrophic cancellation or provide identifiability gating.
While dimensional analysis has been integrated into SR~\cite{tenachi2023,petersen2021},
ADCD extends this with a full five-dimensional (MLT$\Theta$Q) Buckingham-$\pi$ engine,
transcendental argument safety, and BIC-based identifiability reporting
in a single pre-fitting gate cascade.
To our knowledge, ADCD is the first framework to combine deterministic grammar
enumeration, algebraic classical-limit enforcement, phenomenon-specific Bayesian
priors, and explicit identifiability gating for the correction-first paradigm.

\section{Methods}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Problem formulation}

Let $y_\mathrm{obs}$ be the observed target and $y_\mathrm{cl}$ the known
classical prediction. Define the correction residual:
\begin{equation}
  \Delta =
  \begin{cases}
    y_\mathrm{obs}/y_\mathrm{cl} - 1 & \text{(multiplicative)} \\
    y_\mathrm{obs} - y_\mathrm{cl}   & \text{(additive)}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
The multiplicative formulation is adopted when its residual shows weaker
Spearman rank correlation with the classical prediction magnitude than the
additive residual, using a confidence-gated decision boundary that defaults
to additive mode under high ambiguity.
ADCD searches for $\hat{\Delta}(\mathbf{x};\boldsymbol{\theta})$, where
$\mathbf{x}$ is the vector of physical inputs, $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ are
continuous free parameters, and $\hat{\Delta} \to 0$ as the relevant
dimensionless ratio $u \to 0$.

\subsection{Algebraically regularized primitive dictionary}

Each primitive $D(u)$ satisfies $\lim_{u\to 0} D(u) = 0$ as an exact algebraic
identity. This eliminates catastrophic cancellation: the correction vanishes
algebraically, without subtracting large nearly-equal floating-point terms.

Table~\ref{tab:primitives} lists the five primitives.
For \texttt{D\_lor}: in \texttt{float32}, the naive form $(1-u)^{-1/2}-1$
collapses to $0.0$ at $u=10^{-8}$; the regularized form
$u/[\sqrt{1-u}(\sqrt{1-u}+1)]$ returns $5.0\times10^{-17}$ at $u=10^{-16}$
where the naive form returns $0.0$.

\subsection{Five-dimensional Buckingham-$\pi$ ratio generation}

ADCD employs a five-dimensional (MLT$\Theta$Q) Buckingham-$\pi$ engine.
Each variable is represented as a five-vector $\mathbf{d}\in\mathbb{Z}^5$.
The engine constructs dimensionless $\pi$-groups by computing the rational
nullspace of the dimension matrix.

The extension from conventional MLT to MLT$\Theta$Q is crucial for correctness.
For Screened Coulomb, charges $q_1,q_2$ carry genuine Charge dimension Q.
The 5D engine must account for Q in every ratio it generates.
As a result, four parameter slots ($\theta_0$--$\theta_3$) are consumed by
charge-related ratios before the distance-ratio slot $\theta_4$ is reached.
The recovered expression \texttt{theta\_0 * (exp(-r/theta\_4) - 1.0)} carries
subscript 4 as machine-verifiable evidence that the distance ratio was
discovered, not manually supplied: if $r/\theta$ had been hardcoded, the
subscript would be 0.

\subsection{Phenomenon-specific taxonomy as Bayesian prior}
\label{sec:taxonomy}

Each physics phenomenon is associated with a named set of permitted primitive
families, derived exclusively from the fundamental equations of that phenomenon.
\texttt{yukawa\_debye\_screening} permits only \texttt{D\_exp} and \texttt{D\_rat},
because the Klein-Gordon equation yields $V(r)\propto e^{-mr}/r$ and
Debye-H\"{u}ckel theory yields $\phi(r)\propto e^{-r/\lambda_D}/r$.
Trigonometric and power-law families are excluded because no peer-reviewed
derivation of Yukawa or Debye screening produces them.

Crucially, the taxonomy specifies \emph{which functional family}; it does not
specify \emph{which variable enters the argument} or \emph{what the scale parameter is}.
These are determined entirely by the Buckingham-$\pi$ engine and the JAX optimizer.
A physicist confronting a shielding anomaly does not try sinusoidal oscillations
of electric charge: the Klein-Gordon equation rules them out.
ADCD formalizes this prior explicitly, making it documented, auditable, and reproducible.

Adding a physically unjustified primitive (e.g., \texttt{D\_pow} in a
Yukawa scenario) acts as a ``noise sponge'': it absorbs variance from
the distance variable and collapses the Ablation Control BIC gap,
degrading identifiability without improving physical interpretability.
The taxonomy restriction removes hypotheses that physics has already eliminated.

\subsection{Grammar enumeration and theta-intact constraint}

Candidates take the form $\hat{\Delta}=\theta_0\cdot D_i(u_{ij})$,
optionally composed to depth 3 with a secondary term.
Dimensionless arguments are auto-generated by the Buckingham-$\pi$ engine
with a budget of up to 12 candidate ratios per scenario.

All structural gates (Section~\ref{sec:gates}) are evaluated on the
\emph{theta-intact} expression---the fully parameterized form before any
$\theta_i$ are substituted with probe values.
Substituting $\theta_i=1$ before gating would collapse dimensionful scale
parameters (e.g., $r/\theta_4$) into dimensionless constants, falsifying
dimensional analysis.

\subsection{Physical gate cascade}
\label{sec:gates}

Before any numerical fitting, each candidate must pass a five-gate cascade
(Table~\ref{tab:gates}) applied to the theta-intact expression.

\begin{table}[ht]
\small
\caption{The five-gate pre-fitting physical cascade.}
\label{tab:gates}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.5cm}p{9cm}@{}}
\toprule
Gate & Rejection criterion \\
\midrule
1. AST complexity & SymPy tree depth $>12$ or token count $>50$. \\
2. Dimensional consistency & SI base dimensions do not match the target. \\
3. Transcendental safety & Arguments to $\exp$, $\ln$, $\sqrt{\cdot}$ are not dimensionless. \\
4. Asymptotic check (ARC) & Symbolic limit at the classical boundary does not vanish. \\
5. Coarse numerical fitness & Evaluation at $\boldsymbol{\theta}=\mathbf{1}$ produces \texttt{NaN}/\texttt{Inf}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Gate~3 (Transcendental Safety) rejects expressions such as $\exp(q/\theta)$
when $q$ has dimension C, because the argument is not dimensionless.
This prevents dimensionally inconsistent forms from surviving to the optimizer,
even if the optimizer could numerically fit them to acceptable values.

\subsection{Parameter optimization and model selection}

Surviving candidates are optimized via L-BFGS-B in JAX (\texttt{float64}).
Parameters are log-parameterized ($\theta_i = s_i e^{u_i}$) to allow
traversal across orders of magnitude with smooth gradients.

Model selection uses the Bayesian Information Criterion~\cite{schwarz1978}:
$\mathrm{BIC}=k\ln N - 2\ln\hat{L}$.
By the Kass--Raftery scale~\cite{kass1995}, $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}>10$
constitutes very strong evidence; we use this as the minimum threshold
for any identifiability claim.

\subsection{Four-step validation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

A structural discovery claim is made only when all four checks pass:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Budget disclosure.} Full search space size $|\mathcal{C}|$
        and primitive list reported before results are seen.
  \item \textbf{Positive control.} With only the ground-truth primitive,
        the system recovers the correct structure.
  \item \textbf{Ablation control.} With the ground-truth primitive excluded,
        $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}>10$ between the correct and best-alternative structure.
  \item \textbf{Determinism check.} Three independent runs produce
        byte-exact identical results.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Screened Coulomb: exact match proved blind by parameter index.}
The recovered $\theta_0(e^{-r/\theta_4}-1)$ is an exact symbolic match to the
ground truth $\theta_0(e^{-r/\lambda_D}-1)$, with fitted $\theta_4\approx1.50$
matching $\lambda_D=1.50$.
The subscript \texttt{4} rather than \texttt{0} is machine-verifiable evidence
of blind discovery: the 5D Buckingham-$\pi$ engine consumed four parameter slots
($\theta_0$--$\theta_3$) for charge-related ratios before reaching the
distance-ratio slot $\theta_4$.
Had $r/\theta$ been manually supplied, the subscript would be \texttt{0}.
$\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=30.65$ far exceeds the Kass--Raftery threshold.

\paragraph{Entropy Expansion: class-level match with explicit epistemic honesty.}
Recovered $\theta_0\ln(1+dV/V_i)$ matches the ground truth
$\frac{nR}{S_i}\ln(1+dV/V_i)$ at class level: the amplitude $\theta_0$
absorbs the physical ratio $nR/S_i$, which cannot be verified symbolically
without independent measurement of $n$, $R$, and $S_i$.
ADCD explicitly labels this \texttt{class\_only} rather than \texttt{exact}---
epistemic honesty absent in unconstrained SR outputs.
$\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=14.58>10$ supports IDENTIFIABLE.

\paragraph{Time Dilation: WITHHELD as designed.}
At $v\le0.3c$, the relativistic correction is $\le5\%$ of the classical
baseline with 1\% injected noise.
The Lorentz structure $D_\mathrm{lor}(v^2/c^2)$ appears at Rank~1, confirming
the grammar's structural reach.
But the positive control fails (NMSE$=0.41$): even restricted to the correct
primitive family, the optimizer cannot confidently isolate the correction signal
from noise at this historical window.
The system outputs WITHHELD---a correct acknowledgment that the data are not
yet sufficient, mirroring the situation of early-20th-century experimentalists
who observed small deviations from Newton but lacked the precision to confirm
the Lorentz structure.

\section{Discussion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Epistemic strength of the WITHHELD verdict}

The Time Dilation WITHHELD result is frequently misread as failure.
The correct interpretation is the opposite.
A system that returns a confident ``best answer'' regardless of data
sufficiency is epistemically weaker than one that distinguishes between
\emph{found and confirmed} (IDENTIFIABLE), \emph{found but uncertain}, and
\emph{data do not support a claim} (WITHHELD).
ADCD's WITHHELD verdict on Time Dilation at $v\le0.3c$ is the system
correctly diagnosing its own identifiability boundary.
Crucially, the transition from WITHHELD to IDENTIFIABLE is quantitative and
reproducible: better instruments, wider velocity range, or denser data
will push $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}$ above the threshold, and ADCD will report so.

\subsection{Taxonomy as Bayesian prior, not hint}

The distinction between ``hand-feeding the answer'' and ``specifying a
domain-justified prior'' is operational:
the taxonomy restricts \emph{which functional family}---it supplies nothing
about \emph{which variable} enters the argument or \emph{what the scale is}.
A physicist confronting a plasma shielding anomaly does not try sinusoidal
charge oscillations: the Debye-H\"{u}ckel derivation rules them out.
ADCD makes this reasoning explicit, auditable, and reproducible.

Including a physically unjustified primitive in the taxonomy---e.g., adding
\texttt{D\_pow} to a Yukawa scenario---degrades performance: \texttt{D\_pow}
acts as a noise sponge absorbing variance from distance, collapsing the
Ablation Control BIC gap and destroying identifiability without improving
physical interpretability.
The Phenomenon-Specific Taxonomy is not a shortcut; it is the removal of
hypotheses that the founding literature has already falsified.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Positive control sensitivity.}
Time Dilation's positive control failure stems from the weak signal at
$v\le0.3c$, not from an algorithmic defect.
Future work should investigate adaptive restart grids for weak-signal regimes.

\textbf{Scope.}
Current support is limited to five primitives, multiplicative corrections,
and synthetic Gaussian noise.
Extension to additive corrections, heavier-tailed noise, and real
observational data (e.g., SPARC rotation curves for MOND-like corrections)
is a primary direction for future work.

\textbf{Taxonomy coverage.}
Each new taxonomy entry requires documenting a peer-reviewed derivation
for every admitted primitive---a deliberate constraint that prevents
unchecked hypothesis-space expansion.

\section{Conclusion}
% ---------------------------------------------------------------

We presented ADCD, a deterministic, correction-first framework for recovering
physical corrections from observational data.
Three core innovations work in concert:
algebraically regularized primitives that enforce exact classical-limit
vanishing without numerical approximation;
a five-dimensional Buckingham-$\pi$ engine that correctly handles
electromagnetic and thermodynamic variables in automatic ratio generation;
and a Phenomenon-Specific Taxonomy that formalizes domain Bayesian priors
as explicit, auditable constraints on the hypothesis space.

On three canonical scenarios at historical low-signal observation windows,
ADCD delivers two fully validated IDENTIFIABLE results
(Screened Coulomb: $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=30.65$, exact match proved blind;
Entropy Expansion: $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=14.58$, class match with explicit
epistemic qualification)
and one principled WITHHELD verdict (Time Dilation at $v\le0.3c$),
where the system correctly diagnoses that the historical data are insufficient
for a confident structural claim.

The WITHHELD verdict is not a failure; it is ADCD's most important property.
An equation discovery engine that reports uncertainty when data are ambiguous
is more scientifically useful than one that always returns a confident but
unreliable answer.
Within its deliberate scope---known classical baseline, multiplicative correction,
structured Gaussian noise---ADCD provides stronger reproducibility and
identifiability guarantees than general-purpose SR engines,
and furnishes a clear, quantitative signal to the domain expert when those
guarantees cannot be met.
